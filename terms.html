4. Vehicle condition: read this one

Vinyl bonds to your paint. It does not repair it, and it does not hide what is underneath. Film only performs as well as the surface it is applied to.

Before installation we inspect the vehicle and will tell you honestly what we see. You are responsible for disclosing prior bodywork, repaints, filler, rust, or paint that is peeling, cracking, oxidized or aftermarket. On surfaces like these, film may not adhere properly, and removal can lift paint that was already failing.

We are not liable for damage to pre-existing defective, aftermarket or improperly repaired paint, whether that damage appears during installation, during the life of the wrap, or on removal. Where we identify a risk, we will say so before starting, and proceeding is your decision. Factory OEM paint in sound condition is a different matter: normal wrapping and removal is designed not to harm it.

We will give you care instructions. Following them is your responsibility, and warranty coverage assumes you did. In short: let the film cure before washing, hand wash, keep the pressure washer back and off the edges, skip the brush tunnel, and deal with fuel spills, bird droppings and tree sap quickly.
