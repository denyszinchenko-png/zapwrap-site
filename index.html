Car wrap questions, straight answers.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

Properly installed and removed color-change vinyl is generally safe for sound OEM paint. Repainted, repaired, chipped or failing surfaces carry added risk, so we inspect the vehicle first. Vinyl changes the finish; it is not a guarantee against chips or surface damage.

How long does a wrap last?

Service life varies by film, finish, panel orientation, sun exposure, storage and care. We review the manufacturer guidance for the selected film before ordering.

Is it really cheaper than repainting?

A wrap can be a reversible alternative to repainting. Actual cost and removal results depend on the scope, film and condition of the surface underneath.

Can I wrap a leased car?

Often, but the lease agreement controls. Get written permission if required, and have the paint inspected before installation and removal.

How do I wash a wrapped car?

Hand wash or touchless, no wax over matte or satin, and skip the high-pressure lance at the edges. We hand you the full care guide at pickup.

How much does a car wrap cost?

Send the year, make, model, requested coverage and clear photos for an initial estimate. Final written pricing follows vehicle inspection, film selection and confirmed scope. Any requested scope change is priced before work continues.

What films do you install?

We work primarily with 3M, Avery Dennison and KPMF. Other professional films may be considered for a specific finish after availability, suitability and manufacturer guidance are confirmed.

Where does the work happen?

Do you wrap exotics and supercars?

Yes, exotic and high-performance vehicles are welcome. Low clearances, sensors, carbon parts and complex trim require a vehicle-specific inspection and install plan. Disassembly and plotting depend on the exact car and confirmed scope.

How long does a wrap take?

A full color change typically takes 3 to 5 installation days. Vehicle condition, complexity, disassembly and film availability can affect the schedule. Your estimated timeline is included with the confirmed quote.
