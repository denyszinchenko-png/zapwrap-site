Questions before you book.

What should I send before a consultation?

Send the year, make and model, the change you want and clear photos of every relevant side. Close photos of damage, repaired paint or unusual trim help build an accurate scope before the vehicle arrives.

Can every painted or textured part be wrapped?

No. Film needs a suitable surface. Failing clear coat, chips, previous repairs and raw textured plastic can change what is safe or practical, so the vehicle is inspected before the scope is confirmed.
