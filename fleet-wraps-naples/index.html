How a fleet build runs.

  1. Brand intake. Logo files, brand colors, phone and URL, and the one line that has to be readable at a red light. Print-ready art is welcome; no art, we build the layout.
  2. Proof on your vehicle. The layout goes onto a template of your exact make and model, per model in the fleet, and you approve it on screen the way it will sit on the metal.
  3. Print and cut in-house. Graphics print and contour-cut on our own Roland plotter. That is what makes reprints, repairs and next-year additions fast: your files stay on record here.
  4. Install around your schedule. One unit in the bay at a time, drop-offs timed around dispatch, so the routes keep running while the fleet changes clothes.
  5. Handoff with driver rules. A wash guide for the crew: film lives longest when the pressure washer respects the edges.

Coverage is scoped by what the vehicle does and where it parks, not by a package sheet: full wraps, partial wraps with lettering, or clean cut-vinyl lettering and compliance numbers.

Materials built for route work.

Fleet work runs on the same cast films as our color changes: 3M 2080 and Avery Dennison SW900 solids for base color, with printed, contour-cut graphics layered over. Avery's solid line carries the longest maker rating we stock, which matters when a truck has to look right for years without a repaint. KPMF finishes are there when a brand wants a look the competition cannot buy off a shelf. What we do not run is economy roll: a fleet wrap that fades by the second summer costs more than it ever saved.

Fleet wrap questions.

How long is each vehicle off the road?

Installs are staged: one unit in the bay while the rest run their routes, with drop-offs timed around your dispatch. Exact days per unit depend on coverage (full, partial or lettering), and you get that timeline at the consult, in writing.

We add vehicles every year. Will they match?

Yes: your layout files, film codes and vehicle templates stay on record at the studio. Van six matches van one because it prints from the same file onto the same film, not from memory.

What happens at a rebrand or lease return?

The film comes off healthy factory paint clean: that is the point of building a fleet identity in vinyl. Rebrand and refresh the layout, or hand leased units back the way they arrived. The trucks outlive the logo.

Do you design the wrap, or do we bring artwork?

Either. Print-ready files go straight to proof. If all you have is a logo and a phone number, we lay out the wrap for you, and nothing prints until you have approved it on your exact vehicle model.
